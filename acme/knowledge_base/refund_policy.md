# Acme Store Refund Policy

## Refund window
- **Standard customers** can request a refund within **30 days** of delivery.
- **Premium customers** can request a refund within **60 days** of delivery.
- The window starts on the delivery date, not the order date.

## Eligibility
- Orders must have status **delivered** to be refunded.
- Orders that are **pending**, **paid**, or **shipped** should be cancelled instead of refunded.
- Orders that have already been refunded cannot be refunded again.
- Refunds are issued for the full order total unless only some items are returned.

## Exceptions
- Damaged or wrong items are always eligible for a refund, even outside the window, if reported within **90 days** of delivery.
- Opened software, gift cards, and personalized items are not refundable.

## Processing time
- Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within **5–7 business days**.
- Refunds above **$500** require approval from a support supervisor.
