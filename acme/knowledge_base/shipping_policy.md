# Acme Store Shipping Policy

## Delivery options
| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–7 business days | Free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99 |
| Express | 1–2 business days | $14.99 |

## Processing
Orders are processed and shipped within **1–2 business days** of payment.

## Late deliveries
An order is considered **late** if it has not been delivered **10 days** after shipping.
For late orders, support should:
1. Open a high-priority ticket with the carrier investigation.
2. Offer the customer a free replacement or a full refund once the carrier confirms the package is lost.

## Address changes
The delivery address can be changed only while the order status is **pending** or **paid**. Once shipped, the address cannot be changed.
