# Support Team Guidelines

## Ticket priority
- **High:** damaged or wrong items, double charges, lost packages
- **Medium:** refund requests, order changes, products not working
- **Low:** general questions, warranty questions

## Rules for agents (human or AI)
- Always verify the customer's identity by email before sharing order details.
- Never share one customer's information with another customer.
- Always check the refund policy and the customer's tier before approving a refund.
- Refunds above $500 must be escalated to a supervisor, not approved directly.
- If a customer has 3 or more open tickets, escalate to a senior agent.
