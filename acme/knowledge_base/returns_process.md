# How to Return an Item

1. Contact support or open a ticket with your order number.
2. Support confirms the item is eligible under the refund policy.
3. You receive a prepaid return label by email within 24 hours.
4. Pack the item in its original packaging if possible and drop it at any carrier location.
5. Once the warehouse receives and inspects the item (usually 3–5 business days), the refund is issued.

Return shipping is **free** for damaged, defective, or wrong items. For other returns, a **$5.99** return shipping fee is deducted from the refund. Premium customers never pay return shipping fees.
