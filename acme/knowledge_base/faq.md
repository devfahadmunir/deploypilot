# Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I cancel my order?**
Yes, while the order is pending or paid. Once shipped, you'll need to request a return instead.

**Which payment methods do you accept?**
Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and Apple Pay.

**Do you ship internationally?**
Yes, to most countries. International delivery takes 7–14 business days and customs fees are the customer's responsibility.

**I was charged twice. What should I do?**
Open a high-priority ticket. Duplicate charges are reversed within 3–5 business days.

**How do I become a Premium member?**
Spend $1,000 within 12 months and you're upgraded automatically.
