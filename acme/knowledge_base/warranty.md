# Acme Store Warranty

## Coverage
- **Electronics:** 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date, covering manufacturing defects.
- **Home & Kitchen appliances** (kettles, air fryers): 2-year warranty.
- **Clothing, Books, and Sports items:** no warranty beyond the standard refund policy.

## Not covered
Accidental damage, water damage, normal wear and tear, and unauthorized repairs.

## Making a claim
Open a support ticket with the order number, a description of the problem, and a photo or video if possible. Approved claims receive a repair or replacement. If neither is possible, a refund is issued.
