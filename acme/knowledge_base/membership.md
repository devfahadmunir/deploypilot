# Acme Premium Membership

Premium is Acme Store's loyalty tier.

## Benefits
- Extended **60-day** refund window (standard is 30 days)
- Free return shipping on all returns
- Priority support: premium tickets should be answered within **4 hours**
- Free express shipping on orders over $100

## Eligibility
Customers are upgraded to Premium automatically after spending **$1,000** within 12 months.
